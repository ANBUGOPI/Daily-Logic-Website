01

Overview

Daily Logic ("the app," "we," "us") is a solo-developed Android puzzle game published under DiPi. This policy explains what information the app collects when you play, why it's collected, and who it's shared with.

The short version: Daily Logic doesn't ask for your name, email, or phone number. The only data tied to you comes from optional Google Play Games sign-in (for streaks and the leaderboard), your puzzle progress stored in Firebase, and standard ad data if you see an ad served through Google AdMob.

02

Data we collect

What's collected depends on how you use the app:

CategoryWhatWhy
Account (optional) Your Google Play Games profile ID and display name, if you sign in Sync your streak across devices and show your name on the leaderboard
Gameplay data Puzzle results, streak count, completion dates Track your progress and power the streak / leaderboard features
Device & usage App version, device type, crash/diagnostic signals Keep the app stable and fix bugs
Advertising identifiers Google Advertising ID, general ad interaction data Show and measure in-app ads via AdMob

If you don't sign in to Google Play Games, your puzzle progress stays local to your device and isn't linked to any account.

03

Google Play Games

Signing in is optional and unlocks streak sync across devices plus the leaderboard. When you sign in, Google Play Games shares your profile ID and display name with the app, which we then store (via Firebase) alongside your streak and progress. Google's own handling of your Play Games account is governed by Google's Privacy Policy.

You can turn Play Games sign-in on or off in your device's Google Play Games settings at any time.

04

Firebase

Daily Logic uses Firebase (a Google service) as its backend database. When you're signed in, your streak count, puzzle completion history, and leaderboard entry are stored in Firebase, tied to your Play Games account ID.

Firebase may also collect standard diagnostic and crash data to help us identify and fix bugs. This data is handled under Firebase's Privacy and Security policy, which is part of Google's broader privacy commitments.

05

Advertising (AdMob) planned

Daily Logic uses, or will use, Google AdMob to show in-app ads. AdMob may collect your advertising ID and general interaction data (such as which ads are shown and clicked) to serve and measure ads, and — where permitted by your region and consent — to personalize them.

AdMob's data collection and use is governed by Google's Advertising Policy. Where required, the app will present a consent prompt (e.g. via Google's User Messaging Platform) before loading personalized ads.

06

Data sharing

We don't sell your data. Information is shared only with the service providers that make the app work:

  • Google Play Games Services — sign-in, streak sync, leaderboard
  • Firebase — app database and diagnostics
  • Google AdMob — ad serving and measurement

Each of these operates under Google's own privacy policies, linked in the relevant sections above. We may also disclose information if required by law.

07

Retention & deletion

Gameplay data tied to your account is kept for as long as your account is active, so your streak and leaderboard position stay accurate. If you'd like your Firebase-stored data deleted, contact us at the email below and we'll remove it.

08

Children's privacy

Daily Logic is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child has provided us with data (for example, by signing in to Play Games), contact us and we'll delete it.

09

Security

Data stored in Firebase is protected using Google's standard infrastructure security and access-controlled database rules. No method of transmission or storage is 100% secure, but we take reasonable steps to protect your information.

10

Your choices

  • Sign-in: Play Games sign-in is optional — you can play without it, or sign out anytime in your device's Play Games settings.
  • Ad personalization: manage this in your device's Google settings (Settings → Ads) — you can reset or opt out of your advertising ID.
  • Data deletion: email us to request deletion of your Firebase-stored account data.
11

Policy changes

We may update this policy as the app evolves — for example, when ads go live. Material changes will be reflected by updating the "Effective" date at the top of this page.
